# AKA Turbo 3D Grand Prix

Pseudo-3D arcade racer for the Gamebuino AKA (ESP32-S3, 320x240).

- 6 tracks: Sunset Coast, Alpine Pass (hills, snow), Neon City (night, street lights, headlights),
  Palm Bay (sunny tropical bay), Glacier Run (polar night, aurora, ice gorge), Night Harbor (docks, cranes and a bridge by moonlight)
- 6 visual looks: each circuit has its own sky, palette, backdrop, scenery and lap sections
- Circuits are small text files (`tracks/*.trk`) made with the track editor `tools/track_editor.html`:
  see [docs/CIRCUITS.md](docs/CIRCUITS.md) (in French) to add your own
- 7 AI drivers, 4 difficulty levels (Easy / Normal / Hard / Expert)
- Nitro, collisions, off-road slowdown, lap and race records saved to `/sdcard/race3d/`
- Impacts depend on what is hit: bushes and hay are ploughed through, trunks and fences splinter, rocks and buildings stop the car dead, posts and barriers throw sparks, ice shatters, other cars shunt or scrape. Each has its own particles, sound, shake and, for hard hits, a spin-out and smoke
- Engine damage accumulates with every impact (harder and faster hits hurt more): past ~25% the car loses power and top speed, past 50% it pulls to one side and smokes, past 60% the engine cuts out now and then, near the end it burns, and at 100% the engine dies and the race is over (DNF). A health gauge sits next to the nitro one
- Handling (v1.2.0): braking has real bite but needs distance (240 km/h to a stop in about 1.6 s); coasting is slowed by rolling resistance and by aerodynamic drag that grows with the square of speed; a full steering lock at very high speed overloads the front tyres (the car pushes wide and scrubs speed); every upshift cuts the torque for an instant and drops the revs; the car dives under braking and squats under power. On Easy and Normal, red chevrons warn you before a bend that is too sharp for your speed.

**Sound & feel (v1.3.0).** A gearbox "clunk" on each upshift, tyre squeal when the front tyres saturate under steering lock/braking, and wind streaks at the screen edges above 85 % of top speed (full set on nitro).

**Driving coach (v1.4.0).** On easy and normal (offline), one tip per race and topic: "BRAKE BEFORE BENDS" when the corner warning stays red at speed, "GRASS SLOWS YOU DOWN" after a second off the tarmac. Translated in all five languages.
- Pit stop (v1.2): a pit apron opens on the right of the road about 12 segments BEFORE the start line and stays open (no wall) until the second bay, so it is easy to slip in; then a concrete wall separates it from the road (speed limiter 85 km/h). Between the wall and the service lane runs the technicians' safety strip (hazard line, cones); an outer walkway on the far side of the lane is where the two crews wait. Three bays: cyan N2O EXPRESS (refill the nitro, under 95 km/h, no need to stop), yellow TYRES & TRACKING and red ENGINE GARAGE: stop the car (under 4 km/h) and the crew walks in from the outer walkway, goes round the nose (never through the bodywork), kneels at the wheels and works while the lollipop man shows STOP / READY / GO; the repair only advances while they work, then they walk back at once. Hold the gas on GO for a launch boost. Repairs are faster on easier difficulties.
- Cars are imported pictures (`python3 tools/import_car_art.py rear_sheet.png main/game/car_art.cpp three_quarter_sheet.png` turns two sheets of car images on a pink background into `main/game/car_art.cpp`: one true-colour 176x84 rear view and one 224x84 rear three-quarter view per driver). Between the two, 5 intermediate pictures are generated by an optical-flow morph (needs `opencv-python`, `scipy`, `numpy`, `Pillow`); the game picks one according to the steering amount, so turns never shear or twist the car. Lamps and ground contact follow the picture. The 3D-rendered three-quarter views are still in `sprites.cpp` and are used if no picture is loaded
- One chiptune theme per circuit (Sunset Drive, Summit Rush, Neon Nights, Sea Breeze, Polar Lights, Dock Runner), toggled with MUSIC in the title menu
- Cars are imported pictures (see above); the scenery, sounds and music are generated by code at boot. Car shadows are hand-painted 1-bit masks (`tools/car_shadow_sheet.png` -> `python3 tools/import_car_shadows.py tools/car_shadow_sheet.png main/game/car_shadow_art.cpp`), always lit from the upper right
- Online races up to 8 cars (console over Wi-Fi, PC over the internet), player name, 5 languages (see below)

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| D-pad / stick | steer (the stick is analog) |
| A | accelerate |
| B / Down | brake |
| C / D / R1 | nitro (gauge refills slowly) |
| RUN | pause (resume / restart / quit) |
| MENU | AKA system menu: back to main menu, controls, language, credits, back to loader |
| RUN + MENU (hold) | back to loader |

"Back to loader" with the October 2026 launcher (launcher in the `test` partition,
custom bootloader): the bootloader only starts the launcher after a power-on, so
the game powers the console off; switch it back on to get the launcher. With the
older launcher layout (`ota_1`) it jumps there directly.

## Online races (Wi-Fi)

Up to 8 cars per room, shared with friends: the title menu has **ONLINE RACE (WI-FI)**. The public race server
(`aka-turbo-race-server.onrender.com`) is built in; you only need to tell the console your Wi-Fi.

1. Copy `race3d/online.example.txt` to `race3d/online.txt` on the SD card and fill in `ssid=`, `pass=`, `room=` (any name, 10 letters/digits max) and `name=`.
2. Choose ONLINE RACE. The first player in a room is the host: LEFT/RIGHT picks the track, UP/DOWN the number of laps, A starts the race for everybody (empty slots are filled by server-driven computer cars).
3. Friends type the same `room=` on their console, or join from the browser control page (https://jicehel-aka.github.io/Race3D-AKA/).

The free server sleeps when nobody plays, so the first connection can take about a minute. Only the Wi-Fi password is kept on the SD card; it is never sent to the server.
**PC version:** same menu entry, no Wi-Fi setup: it connects through your internet connection to the public server (an optional `online.txt` next to the exe can change `room=`, `host=`...).

**Player name:** choose it in the title menu (PLAYER row, on-screen keyboard, 10 characters); it is saved with the records and shown to the other players. It overrides `name=` in `online.txt`.

**Leaving / fair play:** leaving a race (pause menu) hands your car to the server's computer driver and the others are told "NAME LEFT THE RACE". The server checks every report (speed, acceleration, distance, position): impossible values are clamped, repeated cheating gets the player disqualified.

**Languages:** English, French, German, Spanish, Italian. Console: system menu (language); PC: LANGUAGE row of the title menu or F2. PC keys: F1 menu, F2 language, F11 fullscreen, F12 screenshot (`screenshots/`), Esc on the title screen quits.

Own server: see `server/README.md`, then add `host=your-server.example.com` to `online.txt`.

## Adding a circuit

1. Draw it in `tools/track_editor.html`, download the `.trk` and put it in `tracks/` (`06_my_track.trk`).
2. Run `python tools/gen_tracks.py`.
3. Build as usual.

Existing records are kept: the 1.0 records file (3 circuits) is converted automatically.

## Install with the loader

Copy `sdcard_files/race3d/` to the root of the SD card (`firmware.bin`, `meta.json`,
`screen.bmp`, `lang/`).

## Build

ESP-IDF 5.5.1. `partitions.csv` and `bootloader_components/` come from the official
launcher (20261004), so `idf.py flash` writes the game into `factory` without
touching the launcher.

```
idf.py build
idf.py -p COMx flash monitor
```

The serial log prints the frame rate every 120 frames.

## PC version

`host/` contains an SDL2 version of the same game code, with online play (OpenSSL). On Windows with MSYS2 mingw64
(`pacman -S mingw-w64-x86_64-gcc mingw-w64-x86_64-SDL2 mingw-w64-x86_64-openssl mingw-w64-x86_64-pkgconf`):

```
host/build_pc.sh           # -> host/dist/AKATurbo3D.exe (self-contained), or double-click host/build_pc.bat
```

Linux: `g++ -O2 -std=c++17 -DHOST_BUILD -DHAVE_OPENSSL -I main -I . host/host_main.cpp host/net_pc.cpp main/game/*.cpp $(sdl2-config --cflags --libs) -lssl -lcrypto -lpthread`.
Keys: arrows, X/Space gas, Z brake, C/Shift nitro, Enter/Esc pause, F1 menu, F2 language, F11 fullscreen, F12 screenshot, Esc on the title screen quits. Game pads work too.
Records and settings are saved next to the exe (`race3d_records.bin`, `race3d_lang.txt`).

## Code layout

- `main/main.cpp`: AKA glue (screen, input, audio track, SD card, system menu)
- `main/game/render.cpp`: segment road renderer, parallax sky, sprites, HUD
- `main/game/race.cpp`: car physics, AI drivers, difficulty profiles, laps
- `main/game/track.cpp`: track script parser, scenery, backdrops, themes
- `main/game/tracks_data.cpp`: the circuit list (generated from `tracks/*.trk` by `tools/gen_tracks.py`)
- `tracks/*.trk`: one text file per circuit (source of truth)
- `tools/track_editor.html`: standalone circuit editor (open it in a browser)
- `tools/gen_tracks.py`: rebuilds `tracks_data.cpp` and `TRACK_COUNT`
- `docs/CIRCUITS.md`: how to create, edit and test circuits
- `main/game/sprites.cpp`: procedural pixel art (cars, trees, buildings...)
- `main/game/synth.cpp`: engine / tyre / effect sound synthesizer
- `main/game/i18n.cpp`: all texts in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian (`TR("ENGLISH TEXT")`)
- `main/game/online.cpp`, `ws.cpp`: online client (binary protocol, lobby, remote cars, names); `main/net_esp.cpp` console Wi-Fi + TLS transport; `host/net_pc.cpp` PC transport (OpenSSL)
- `main/game/car_art.cpp`, `car_shadow_art.cpp`: generated car pictures and shadow masks (see `tools/import_*.py`)
- `main/game/tech_art.cpp`: pit crew technician pictures imported from `tools/technician_sheet.png` (`python3 tools/import_technicians.py tools/technician_sheet.png main/game`)
- `server/`: the race server (Node, Render), `site/`: the browser control page (GitHub Pages)
